The Founder

Beena John Balaji
The Visionary

Beena's Closet was born from the passion of Beena John Balaji, who curates and creates fine, high-end handcrafted jewelry. Balancing her life between Mumbai, India and the United States, Beena brings a rich global perspective and a deep appreciation for heritage to each design.

Every piece in our jewelry collection is a masterpiece of devotion — meticulously designed with premium stones, royal Kundan craft, and brilliant gold plating to complement your elegance. Our collections bring the grandeur of traditional royalty together with the sleek minimalism of contemporary luxury.

Serving discerning clients across the globe, we are committed to making fine designer jewelry accessible, elegant, and timeless.
